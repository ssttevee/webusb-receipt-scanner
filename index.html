Receipt Scanner

Scan documents and convert them to 1-bit PDFs with live preview

Instructions & Keyboard Shortcuts

How to use:

  1. Click "Connect Scanner" and select your USB scanner
  2. Adjust binary threshold and trim settings as needed
  3. Click "Scan Document" to start scanning
  4. Place documents on scanner - they will be processed automatically
  5. Use "Download PDF" to save all scanned pages as a single PDF

Keyboard Shortcuts:

  • Ctrl+S - Start scanning
  • Ctrl+Enter - Download PDF
  • Ctrl+Delete - Clear all pages
  • Escape - Stop scanning
